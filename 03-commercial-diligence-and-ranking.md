## 1. Why Existing Tools Fail This Demographic (The "Hidden Niche" Wedge)

Generic tools force high-performing women into a false choice:
- **Enterprise/Dev Tools (`Notion`, `Asana`, `Motion`, `Sunsama`):** Built around single-track corporate calendars. They treat a LinkedIn video or Instagram Reel like a text task, have zero awareness of **cross-OS media friction** (shooting 4K video/voice notes on an iPhone/iCloud while working on a Windows corporate PC with OneDrive/Outlook or Google Drive), and ignore **"Camera-Ready vs. Deep-Exec Energy"** scheduling.
- **Social Schedulers (`Later`, `Buffer`, `Hootsuite`):** Disconnected from executive deliverables, board reports, and personal/family non-negotiables. Worse, they try to proxy/store heavy video files on their own servers, leading to broken uploads, compressed quality, and clunky mobile-to-PC workflows.

**Our Unfair Technical & Commercial Wedge ("Zero-Blob Metadata Pointer Index"):**
Instead of paying AWS/Cloudflare to upload, transcode, and store gigabytes of user videos (which kills margins and triggers 6-month Google CASA / Meta API security audits), **Aurelia OS and its 3 spin-offs store `<15 KB` CRDT metadata pointers + `<25 KB` client-generated WebP micro-thumbnails** that deep-link directly into the user's native **iCloud Drive, Google Drive, and Microsoft OneDrive**.
- **Gross Margin:** **92%–96%** (`<$0.15/mo` total infra + AI cost per active user; `<$35/mo` fixed base for the first 2,500 users).
- **Platform Footprint:** Single **Vite SPA + Capacitor 8 (iOS & Android) + Windows WebView2/PWA + Node/SQLite** monorepo — already proven on this workstation (`C:\dev\ndv` toolchain).
