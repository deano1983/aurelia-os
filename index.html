How it works

Three moves a day. No migration, no new storage bill.

1

Index, don't ingest

Aurelia records a sub-15 KB pointer plus a thumbnail for each asset and deep-links back with icloud://, gdrive:// or onedrive://. Your 4K video and board PDFs stay exactly where they are.

2

Plan the real day

Hard executive deadlines, publishing cadence and personal non-negotiables share one chronological timeline. Collisions are flagged before they bite, with a one-tap cascade that only moves flexible work.

3

Hand off in two taps

The caption, proof asset and aspect ratio arrive in the native app ready to post. For executives, the EA attaches the proof and the exec approves from the phone — nobody chases anybody.

Questions worth asking

Before you connect a cloud

Do you store my video, board packs or client files?

No. Aurelia keeps a metadata pointer (under 15 KB) and a small thumbnail, then hands you back a deep link into iCloud, Google Drive or OneDrive. The raw file is fetched by your own device from your own account. That is why there is no storage tier and no egress charge.

Which platforms actually work today?

The workspace demo runs in any modern browser and installs as a PWA on iOS, Android and Windows. The cloud pickers use Google Drive drive.file, the OneDrive file picker and the iOS Capacitor EventKit / photo-library bridge, so no six-month provider security audit is triggered.

Can I put this on a corporate card?

Yes for Velvet Cadence, Sovereign Brief and the Aurelia OS suite. The workspace generates an Australian GST tax invoice (ABN 61 794 639 443) coded to Professional Development / Executive Communications & PR Software, plus a one-click finance approval memo. What happens if I start on a spin-off and later want the suite?

All four doors share the same cloud_assets and command_items schema, so the upgrade is a plan change rather than a migration. Your pointers, items and history stay exactly where they are.

Can I see the numbers before I buy?

Yes — the commercial deck carries the unit economics, the 30-day sprint model and a live MRR simulator. The 100-client CRM board and the outreach console are public too: this is a working build, not a waitlist.

How do I get support?

Open an issue on the project repository. Payment, invoice and refund questions are handled by the merchant of record that processed the transaction.
